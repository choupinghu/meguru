Collect the country, one region at a time

Every charm is a postcard from somewhere in Japan.

Gotochi (ご当地) Hello Kitty are made region by region — apples in Aomori, gold leaf in Kanazawa, a shīsā in Okinawa. Meguru maps a personal collection onto the country it came from, so you can wander it the way you'd wander Japan. The map lights up only where charms actually live.

Why Meguru

Meguru (巡る) means to make the rounds — to travel a circuit and come back changed. Collectors gather these charms for the joy of it, but rarely get to see the shape of what they've gathered. Meguru turns a shelf into a map: where a charm was made, what it depicts, and the condition it's in.

Everything here is genuine and legally sourced. Pieces come in a range of states, so condition is labelled plainly on every card — no surprises.

Interested in a piece? Enquiry-only for now — buying is casual (bank transfer / PayLah), arranged one-to-one. Payment tools come later, once the shelf finds its people.
